What these words mean
Every bet here is a moneyline: a bet that a team wins the game outright (tennis: wins the match). MONEYLINE, SPREAD and TOTAL are Pinnacle's own prices (decimal odds), shown so you can see the whole board; hands-free bets only the moneyline. Fair is Pinnacle's price with the bookmaker's margin removed; Novig is what Novig sells it for; edge is how much better Novig's price is than fair. A pick is a $1 order up to the limit shown.
