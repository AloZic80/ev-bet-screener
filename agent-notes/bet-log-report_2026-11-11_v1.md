# Bet log report (CSV of Oct 24 - Nov 10, 2026)

Ref: CC-2026-11-11-06 · Written by Claude Code, Wed 2026-11-11, America/Chicago.

This report describes what happened in the bet log. It is not advice to bet more, less or differently. Every number below was worked out by one analyst and then worked out again from scratch by a second checker. Where the two disagreed, the checker's corrected figure is used.

A few words used throughout:
- **Single**: one bet on its own. **Parlay**: several bets joined into one ticket.
- **Model fair chance**: the "fair %" saved with the bet, turned into a chance. It is the model's own view, before La Contadora makes it more careful.
- **Price chance**: the chance the price you paid implies. It still includes the book's cut (the vig), so it is the break-even rate, not the true chance.
- **P**: how often a result this far off (or further) would happen by luck alone if the stated chances were right. A P of 0.30 is ordinary. A P under about 0.01 is rare.
- **Under 100 bets**: too small to show anything on its own. These are marked.

## What this data cannot tell you yet

- **The careful chance is missing.** This older CSV has no La Contadora chance and no no-vig market chance. Every test here is against the model's raw fair chance or the price paid (vig included). It says nothing about how La Contadora's chance would have scored, and that is the one that drives every decision. A new CSV export from v4.68 has both columns.
- **The break-even test is not a true-chance test.** The price chance includes the book's cut, so "won as often as the price said" means "about break-even", not "the edge is real".
- **Sample sizes.** 288 decided singles is a start. Almost every group (each sport except NHL, each book except Novig, every band, every bucket, the sacks, the parlays) is under 100 bets and cannot show anything on its own. The Scorecard's 1,000-bet test is the right yardstick; this log is under a third of the way there.
- **Many looks.** 41 group win tests were run, plus bands and buckets. Some low P values are expected by chance, and the groups overlap.
- **Cuts chosen after the fact.** The "under 45%" long-shot line and the smallest-stake bucket were picked after seeing results, which makes them look more surprising than they are.
- **30 settled singles have no fair price saved** and are left out of every model comparison (they went 16 wins against 14.49 expected at the price).
- **The Browns pair** (Nov 1, +178, two $1.05 tickets) needs your confirmation: two real tickets, or one entered twice?
