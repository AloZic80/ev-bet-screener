So the bands are studied in steps, with each guess written down before the bets that test it.

## The rules (frozen now)
1. **The official bands stay 5 points wide** (calibration-v1: Unlikely <45, Coin Flip 45–50, On the Line 50–55, Slight Lean 55–60, Lean 60–65, ...).
   They are the record. They are never redrawn from results.
2. **The study starts on 11/11** (Alonzo's choice), with Scorecard bets only (`inScorecard`), and La Contadora's chance saved with the bet before kickoff (`snap.act`).
   The plan was set on 11/15 00:40 CT, so Block 1 starts with 32 settled bets (+$32.68) already known: a small head start, shown in the app.
3. **Explore (first 500 settled study bets):** the app's Reports › By › "Band, 2.5% steps (exploratory)" shows finer slices, with the Read word and the
   many-group check. Anything seen there is a lead, not proof.
4. **Write the guess down:** at 500 settled bets, Alonzo (with Sally and El Capataz) writes ONE guess, e.g. "51–54% earns more than the rest of 50–60%",
   with its exact edges, in agent-notes, dated, BEFORE the next bets.
5. **Test the guess on the NEXT bets only** (the rest of Block 1 and Block 2), without changing it. It passes only if, on those new bets alone, its
   ROI range clears zero (the Scorecard's rule) and it beats the rest of the same 5-point band.
6. **Only if it passes** may the app get new bands, as a new version (bands-v2), with v1 kept for history. A failed guess is written down too.
7. **The blocks:** Block 1 = 1st–1,000th Scorecard bet placed since 11/11 (starts the record), Block 2 = 1,001–2,000 (confirms), Block 3 = 2,001–3,000
   (reconfirms). Each is judged on its own with the Scorecard's rules, which stay frozen inside a block.
8. Nothing in this plan changes a stake, the floor or a BET / SKIP. Bands and tabs are labels.
