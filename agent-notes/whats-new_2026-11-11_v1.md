## 2. ✅ La Contadora now says YES or NO, plainly

No more "missed your floor by 1.3 pts" riddles:

- Every **Place these** list says: **"✅ La Contadora: YES. The math says bet every card here."**
- A near-miss card says: **"🍀 La Contadora: NO · just under your floor (+0.8% vs your 1.5%) · optional, smallest bet"**.
  - That means the edge is real but smaller than your floor: if you want it, the smallest bet, up to 5 a day.

## 3. 🏀 Live Games finds your Novig bets

Your AJ Dybantsa Under 23.5 Pts+Reb showed "Game not found on ESPN · Not started" while ESPN had WSH 101 – IND 114 in the 4th.

- **Why:** Novig describes a player bet only as "AJ Dybantsa 23.5 POINTS_REBOUNDS", with no teams. So the app never knew which game it was.
- **Now:** Live Games finds the game the way the grader does, by looking for the player in ESPN's box scores of the games that started around Novig's start time.
  - It only takes the game when exactly one game matches; otherwise it doesn't guess.
  - You'll see the live score and the stat bar ("20 · room 3.5 · projection 22.4").
- Bets with no game no longer pile into one "Game not found" box, and they say "Game not known yet" instead of a wrong "Not started".

## 4. 🐛 A grading bug caught before it cost you a result

While testing #3 we found that Live Games could **settle a bet from a box score read mid-game**.

- In the test, an Over 27.5 was marked **lost** at 20 points. The final was 28, a **win**.
- **Fixed:** a finished game is now settled only from ESPN's **final** box score, as the regular grader always did.

## 6. ⏱ Coming right after: v4.77, the "Old" tab (your idea)

"Put the older bets in another category … If sentinels takes them out of jail, then good."
