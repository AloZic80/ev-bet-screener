## B. Sweet spot (owner, 11/10)
> "Sweet spots will always be on the Safe bets, since they are safe to bet because of the chances I got to win."

- 🎯 SWEET SPOT only ever goes on best bets, never on Just missed or Hail Marys.
- Proposed: judge it on La Contadora's EV, not the model's. Eklund's "+6.3%" sweet spot is +3.1% for her.
- The odds range today is −150 to +150, which includes bets that win only 40% of the time (+150 = 40%). If "safe" means winning more often than losing, the range would start at −100 (50%). The owner decides.

## C. PERFECTA ring (proposal, name to be chosen)
- **Six checks:**
  1. zone 50–75%;
  2. La Contadora's EV +4% to +12%;
  3. room for error ≥ 2.5 points;
  4. model ≥ market > price;
  5. price seen ≤ 10 min ago;
  6. clean (no injury, weather, outlier, moved line, same player, or capped stake).
- **Look** (owner's ideas):
  - the card's background changes and says **BET NOW** while the price is fresh;
  - 5 of 6 get a thin ring that names the missing check.
- **Parlay leg tag** (owner: "some that are just better to use as parlays, since they don't pay as much"): 70%+ favorites at −230 or shorter. They pay little alone, but on a pick'em app every pick pays the same, so a 72% pick against roughly 56% needed per pick is worth more there.
- **Records:** logged bets keep the ring, and Performance compares ringed bets with the rest.
