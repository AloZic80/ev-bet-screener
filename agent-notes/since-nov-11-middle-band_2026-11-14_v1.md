## 6. What to keep doing so the bets count

- **Log every bet, won or lost, when you place it.** Only settled bets count; an open bet counts once it is graded.
- **Save it from its card with "I bet this".** That saves La Contadora's chance (`snap.act`), the price, the tab and the app's call with the bet. Since
  11/11, 40 of 50 bets have La Contadora's chance and 10 have none (all 10 SKIP). A bet with no forecast still counts on the Scorecard, but it has no band
  and no expected profit, so it cannot test the forecast.
- **Book fills.** A fill the app logs by itself gets a forecast only if its card is still on the board when the fill is read. Pressing "I bet this" when you
  place it avoids that. Recovering the forecast from the price history is agreed and not built yet (CC-2026-11-14-11 and -12).
- **Closing prices.** The CLV half of the test needs them, and only 168 of the 322 have one. The app saves one when it reads a live price in the 45 minutes
  before the start, or when you press "Grab closing lines" (best 5–15 minutes before the game; 1 API call per game). You can also type one in the Bet log.
- **Log the bets the app said SKIP too, if you place them.** The call saved with each bet lets the record be split later into "the app's call" and "my own picks".
- **Keep the rules frozen inside a block**, and note the day if one changes.
- Parlays, research bets and extras (Just missed, Hail Marys) stay outside the 1,000.
